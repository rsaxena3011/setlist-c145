### R-TD (stop-loss bound, diagnostic, uncounted) — CORRECTED (R1 fix, the largest correction in this file)

**The draft's table was wrong.** The stop-loss computation used `max(payoff_0, -s)` — the stopped-miss payoff was -s WITHOUT the 0.30 round-trip fee, which the hosts charge on EVERY trade including stopped-out ones. The correct stopped-miss payoff is `-s - 0.30`. The f* formula was also wrong in two places: it used EV(0) in the numerator (the winners-only model starts from the optimistic bound EV_opt, not the no-stop EV) and (tp - s) in the denominator (the per-dipped-winner loss is (tp-1-0.3) - (-s-0.3) = tp-1+s — the fee cancels). Corrected: **f* = EV_opt / [p_T · (tp - 1 + s)]**, where EV(f) = EV_opt - p_T·f·(tp-1+s) and f* is the dip fraction at which the optimistic bound breaks even. Fixed in `stageB/rerun_tests.py` (payoff + f* + note) and the full script re-run.
